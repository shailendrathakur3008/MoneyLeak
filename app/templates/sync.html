Supported Financial Integrations & Privacy

1. Account Aggregator (AA) Ecosystem Regulated by RBI under NBFC-AA Master Directions. Provides secure, read-only data sharing with user explicit OTP consent. Never requests net banking passwords, ATM PINs, or UPI PINs.
2. Universal UPI & Card Coverage UPI payments initiated through Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or merchant checkout settle in the linked bank account and are recognized accurately by narration analysis.
3. Deduplication & Zero-Double-Counting Credit card bill payments (CRED, net banking transfers) are treated as transfers, and mutual fund SIPs are mapped to Savings Contributions, never as consumption expenses.

Consent & Data Protection Policies

• Zero Credential Storage: MoneyLeak never asks for or stores your net banking password, debit card PIN, UPI MPIN, or banking OTP.

• Strict Read-Only Mode: The integration layer is strictly read-only for transaction tracking and cannot execute payments or move funds.

• Instant Revocation: Disconnecting any source immediately revokes the Account Aggregator consent token and severs all API communication.
